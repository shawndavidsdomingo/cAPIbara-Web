Up and running in three steps

Install

Download the app, run pip install -r requirements.txt and start it with python main.py.

Create your account

Sign up and confirm your email with a six-digit code. Then log in with your username or email.

Add your keys, if you like

Paste a Brevo or TextBee key into a .env file to send for real. Without keys, the app runs in demo mode.

Built to be trusted with your stuff

Small app, careful basics.

Passwords never stored as typed

Each password is kept as a random salt and a PBKDF2-SHA256 hash. Forgot yours? A one-time code goes to the email on the account.

Your data stays on your computer

Links, drafts, contacts and generated data live in a local database next to the app. Other accounts cannot see yours.

Easy on the eyes

Zoom the whole app with Ctrl and the mouse wheel, or Ctrl with plus, minus and zero. It remembers your size.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. cAPIbara is a free desktop app. The outside services it connects to may have their own limits or plans.

Do I need accounts with Brevo or TextBee?

Only if you want to send real emails or texts. Without keys, the app still works in demo mode and nothing leaves your computer.

Can I text any number?

Texts support Philippine mobile numbers in the 09XXXXXXXXX format, and they send from the Android phone linked to your TextBee account.

Does "sent" mean the message arrived?

Not always. It means the service accepted it. Delivery depends on the phone, the network and the carrier, and emails can land in spam.

Can I get my data out?

Yes. Generated data exports to Excel, CSV or JSON. Everything else sits in a local database file you control.

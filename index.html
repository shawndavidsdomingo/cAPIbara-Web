Four tools, one login

Sign in once and move between tools from the sidebar. Your links, drafts and settings belong to your account only.

URL Shortening

  • Paste a full link and get a short one from the Ulvis service.
  • Shorten the same link twice and it moves to the top instead of duplicating.
  • Click any link in your history to copy it, or press Open to visit it.
  • Search by short or original link, and sort by short link, original link or date.
  • The window never freezes, even when the connection is slow or offline.

SMS and Email

  • Write messages in separate SMS and Email tabs.
  • Save drafts, reopen them later, and see a Sent list.
  • A live counter shows the 160-character SMS limit.
  • The app asks before you leave with unsaved text.

Fake Data Generator

  • Create 10 to 1,000 sample people per batch, each with 10 fields such as name, address, birthdate and email.
  • Keep a history of batches, and delete one or clear them all.
  • Sort any column, then export exactly what you see to Excel.

Accounts and Profile

  • Register with an email code before the account is created.
  • Passwords are stored as a random salt and a PBKDF2-SHA256 hash, never as plain text.
  • Change your name, email or password from the Profile page.
  • A dashboard shows your totals: emails, texts, links and generations.

Where the project stands

cAPIbara is built in weekly sprints. About half of it is done.

Working now

  • Sprint 1: accounts, login, SQLite storage, secure password hashing
  • Sprint 2: Ulvis link shortening with saved history, search, sort and click copy
  • Dashboard, profile editing and the SMS/Email draft screens
  • Fake data generator with sorting and Excel export

Coming next

  • Sprint 3: real email and SMS providers (today, sending is a demo and nothing leaves the app)
  • Sprint 4: fake data and message counters saved in the database
  • Interface polish: show/hide password, inline field errors, smaller-screen sizing
